**Badge** — the brand's genre tag and small status label.

```jsx
<Badge variant="gold">Trabi-Komödie</Badge>
<Badge variant="plum">Märchen-Komödie</Badge>
```

Every show carries a hyphenated genre tag — that's a brand signature. `variant`: `gold` (default/primary genre), `plum`, `neutral`, `outline`, `stage` (on dark surfaces). Text is auto-uppercased.
