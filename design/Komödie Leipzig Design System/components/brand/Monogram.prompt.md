**Monogram** — the proscenium-arch mark; use as favicon, app icon, avatar, loading mark, or inside the Wordmark lockup.

```jsx
<Monogram size={48} tone="gold" />
```

- `tone`: `gold` (dark backgrounds), `ink` (light backgrounds), `mono` (inherits `currentColor` for one-color print).
- Keep clear space of at least 25% of its size around it.
