**Wordmark** — the primary Komödie Leipzig logo; use it in headers, footers, login screens, share cards, and any brand moment.

```jsx
<Wordmark layout="stacked" tone="gold" tagline size="lg" />
```

Variants:
- `layout`: `stacked` (mark above name — hero / poster), `horizontal` (header / nav), `wordmark-only` (tight spaces).
- `tone`: `gold` on dark stage surfaces (`.kl-stage`, plum, ink); `ink` on light parchment.
- `tagline`: adds the "Bei uns lachen Sie besser" strapline.
- The "Ö" umlaut is rendered as two gold footlights — never replace it with a plain Ö.
